50-30-20 Budget Rule

The golden rule of personal finance. Allocate your UPI income smartly across Needs, Wants, and Savings.

₹25,000
Needs (50%)
Rent, groceries, utilities, transport, healthcare
₹15,000
Wants (30%)
Dining, entertainment, shopping, hobbies
₹10,000
Savings (20%)
Emergency fund, investments, debt repayment

📊 UPI Spending Allocation Guide

🍔 Food: Needs (if groceries) / Wants (if dining out)
🚗 Transport: Needs (commute) / Wants (cab for leisure)
🛒 Shopping: Wants (discretionary)
💡 Utilities: Needs (essential bills)
🎬 Entertainment: Wants (subscriptions, events)
💊 Health: Needs (medicines, insurance)
